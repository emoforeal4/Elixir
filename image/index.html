01 — ABOUT

Ten years behind two different lenses.

I spent the first half of my career editing brand films — learning how a well-timed cut, a slow push-in, or a held frame can carry more feeling than a dozen lines of copy. The second half has been spent building for the browser, where I found that scroll position is just another timeline.

Now I sit between the two: designing and developing sites that borrow their pacing from cinema — depth, parallax, and restraint — and their structure from good front-end engineering.
